The ten SFX MP3s in `public/projects/automatika-fast-ad/sfx/` are original synthesized audio, generated by `generate_sfx.py`. The script uses deterministic oscillator sweeps and seeded, filtered noise. It incorporates no downloaded samples and refuses to replace existing files. Each mono 44.1 kHz MP3 is encoded with FFmpeg at 192 kb/s, with source PCM normalized to about -6.7 dBFS peak before the per-cue Remotion volume.
